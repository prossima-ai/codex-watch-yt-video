Candidate decisions this prototype exposes

  • Keep JPEG as the ordinary frame format and 768 px as the default maximum width; re-extract a specific frame at 1024 px when small text or fine spatial detail is material.
  • Inspect every selected frame in chronological batches of eight. Twelve worked in the fixture, but eight leaves room for transcript and reasoning while preserving accuracy.
  • Keep frame timestamps absolute. Merge cue and ordinary frames before batching, and label each frame’s selection reason.
  • Keep speech and visual claims separate. A cue can request a frame, but transcript wording cannot substitute for visible evidence.
  • Reuse already-inspected frames and transcript for same-video follow-ups in the same task; reacquire only when the new question requires unsampled evidence.
  • Retain the parity baseline’s selection caps—0 ordinary frames for transcript, 50 for efficient, 100 for balanced, and no default cap for token-burner—because this fixture found no Codex-specific reason to change their meanings. Treat batching as an ingestion strategy, not a lower selection cap.
Calibration boundary: the blind run validated up to 12 images per inspection batch, not complete 50-, 100-, or 251-frame watch requests. The final acceptance matrix must include those scale cases before release; this prototype does not pretend to prove them.
